Native EA SPORTS UFC 3 port (PS4 `CUSA06534`, update 1.14) for Windows and Linux.
**No game files are included**: you need your own dump of the game and its 1.14 update.

**Hotfix for 0.1.11: the game could close when a trophy was unlocked.** Everyone on 0.1.11
should update. How to play online:
[HOW-TO-PLAY-ONLINE.md](https://github.com/GNSB/UFC-3-PS4-PORT-LINUX-WINDOWS/blob/main/docs/ufc3/HOW-TO-PLAY-ONLINE.md).

**Extract this release into a new, empty folder.** Do not extract it over or inside an older
version. Your saves and `trophies.json` stay in the data folder.

### Fixes

- **Crash when a trophy notification appeared** (new in 0.1.11). The notification passed its
  icon to the graphics driver the wrong way, so the driver read garbage. It could close the game
  as soon as a trophy was unlocked, for example when signing a Career contract. Trophies were
  always saved, even when it crashed.
  - If you turned "Trophy notifications" off as a workaround, you can turn it back on.
  - `BB_TROPHY_TEST=1` shows two test notifications at start (nothing is unlocked or saved), to
    check that they work on your PC.

### Known issues

- In Quick Fight, blood can show on the face but not on the body or the mat (Career is fine).
  A fix is being tested.
- With `BB_WRITE_WINDOW=2`, blood and sweat still do not show (#35). The default (mode 1) is not
  affected.
- CPUs without BMI1/MOVBE/LZCNT (Intel Core 2nd/3rd gen) are still not supported. PR #48 is under
  review.
